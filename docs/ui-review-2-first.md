# UI review 2: first impressions, home screen and navigation

Fresh review, November 2026. Built to a scratch folder, server at 10x time scale, Playwright at 1280×720 (desktop) and 1024×768 (iPad). Screenshots were saved to the reviewer's scratchpad (`rev2-first/`), and the names below refer to those files.

## Verdict

**What's good, keep it.** The brand lands straight away: the hazard tape, the chunky display font, "Get on the bus", "G'day Jack" and the Aussie badge names (Pie Burner, Fair Dinkum Start, Vegemite Glaze) give it a voice that Zwift and TrainerRoad don't have. Login is a Netflix-style "Who's riding?" grid, and the password box focuses itself once you pick a face, which is right for a group of seven mates (`01-first-load`, `02-picked-kat`). The welcome tour is short (three steps), has Back, asks for FTP with a sane default and offers the simulator (`04-tour-0..2`). The live trainer chip in the header ("Simulated trainer · 152 W · 91 rpm") is good: one glance tells you the trainer is alive (`06-home-full`). The bus lobby is the best screen in the app: a big code, the seats, a workout profile chart, effort presets showing real watts, and one huge START THE BUS (`12-zb-click`). The Garage has proper 3D renders and the sticky START bars on Free ride and Solo are good (`08-tab-Garage`, `10-free`, `23-ipad-solo`). The results screen ("MADE IT!", awards for crew mates, scrap earned) has the right energy (`15-end`).

**What holds it back.** The home screen is a static menu of eleven same-weight emoji tiles. It never changes, whoever you are and whatever you did yesterday: after a ride that earned 267 scrap it looks exactly as it did on first run (`16-returning-home` vs `06-home-full`). It doesn't answer the two questions a rider has when they clip in: "what should I ride today?" and "who's on right now?". The live, social part (Open rides) sits below the fold (`18-home-open-ride`). The ten minutes after the tour are a choice wall with no "start here". Plumbing also adds friction: the trainer choice isn't remembered across a reload, the URL never changes (no deep links, browser Back does nothing useful) and the crew games are emoji on grey cards where they deserve posters.

## Top five to do first

1. Today hero card (1). 2. Open rides at the top with a loud Join (2). 3. Remember the trainer (3). 4. Tour ends in a bot bus run (4). 5. Posters for the crew games (5). Ideas 2, 3 and 4 are small and together fix most of the first-ten-minutes and returning-rider friction. Ideas 1 and 5 are what make the home screen feel like a game.
