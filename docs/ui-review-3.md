# UI review 3: the new crew games, Zombie Bus extras, home and Rider tab

Reviewed 15 November 2026 as a player, at 1280×800 (desktop) and 1180×820 (iPad landscape), on a fresh database with the simulator trainer and three bots per game. The server ran at `ZB_TIME_SCALE` 20 (8 for the second Zombie Bus ride), so the simulator's lag, very short quiz timers and banners that seem to hang about longer than usual may partly be side effects of the speed-up. Screenshots are in `docs/screenshots/review3/` (`desk-…` and `ipad-…`).

I couldn't review the servo stop's upgrade shop. This week's Zombie Bus modifier is **Closed Servos** ("The servo shop stays shut all ride"), so it never opened in either ride.

## Verdict

There's a lot of good stuff here. The Zombie Bus HUD is rich, the route vote is clear, the awards are funny and the lobbies get you riding in three taps. The five new games are the weak spot: they feel like the Zombie Bus with a different panel bolted on. Every one of them defaulted to the *Bus Crew 45* workout, so you ride "Long haul", "Servo stop" and "Horde surge" blocks, get a red **FAIL!** for a servo sprint in a pub quiz, and the results page is titled "Bus Crew 45". The game itself barely shows in the 3D world (no fox in The Hunt, no team colours in the Team Time Trial, knocked-out riders keep pedalling in the bunch). Results lead with the crew's best number as if it were yours. Give each game its own workout, its own words and one visible thing on the road, and they'll feel like five new games instead of five HUD widgets.

## Bugs

1. **Results show the crew's best as your score**: Pub Quiz ("Top score 237" for a rider on 0), Last Rider Standing ("Highest step 78" for a rider out at 60), TTT and The Hunt (`ipad-pub-quiz-4-results`, `desk-last-rider-standing-4-results`).
2. **Results are titled with the workout name** ("Bus Crew 45") instead of the game, for Pub Quiz, Galley Ship, The Hunt and TTT. The feed also lists Zombie Bus rides as "Bus Crew 45".
3. **Garage bike thumbnails frame the rider's head, not the bike** (Fairy Floss, Murdered Out, Chrome Dome, Indoor Escapee). Deal of the Week shows the Murdered Out bike as a face, and it appears twice (deal and Treat yourself) (`desk-garage2`).
4. **Last Rider Standing knocked out an ERG-locked rider on step 1** (target 120 W, power 110 W, out at 60%), then gave the same rider "Iron Lungs, 93% on target" (`desk-last-rider-standing-3-ride-01`). This may be partly the speed-up, but ERG lag shouldn't knock anyone out.
5. **Galley Ship HULL shows 0 from the start**, and "On the beat" shows raw seconds (1658 s).
6. **Overlays overlap**: FAIL!, SUPPLY DROP and the sprint line sit on top of the route vote card (`desk-bus-vote`).
7. **iPad Zombie Bus**: the bus dashboard covers the Stats and camera buttons, and the REPELLENT label runs into Stats (`ipad-zombie-bus-3-ride-01`).
8. **iPad Pub Quiz**: the answer card overlaps the feed, and its "20 s" timer wraps onto two lines (`ipad-pub-quiz-3-ride-05`).
9. The route vote says "when the servo closes" during the Closed Servos week.
10. Login logs a 401 and a 404 in the console (harmless, but noisy).

## What already works

- **Lobbies**: the bus-shaped crew card, one-tap bots, the "3 of 3 ready" start button and big letter codes are quick and friendly.
- **Zombie Bus HUD**: the horde-gap bar, armour, nitro letters and the rear-view inset tell a story at a glance. The **Which way next?** vote is the best new piece, with three clear cards, 1/2/3 keys and real trade-offs (`desk-bus-vote`).
- **Weather** looks great: rain streaks and dusk light make the same road feel different.
- **Last Rider Standing** has a 👑 crown and a big "Next: 204 W · 2" countdown that are exactly the right drama (`desk-last-rider-standing-3-ride-02`).
- **Awards are funny and Australian**: Knows Nothing, Fell Off King, Drummer's Pet, Metronome. The scrap banner with "Spend it in the Garage →" closes the loop nicely.
- **Home**: the shelves (Chase / Party / Hard efforts) are a good way to sort the games. "Today and this week" gives a reason to ride, and the crew feed with 👏 kudos feels social.
- **Garage**: hat thumbnails on your own rider are charming, the "N to go" progress bars are motivating, and "Treat yourself: you can afford these now" is a smart nudge.
- **Rider tab**: the level badge, time-in-zone bar and monthly wrap read cleanly on iPad.
