# Plan from the first real rides (2026-11-14)

The crew rode Zombie Bus and a free-ride route on real trainers for the first time. These are their notes, turned into a checklist. Work top to bottom within each group; groups are in priority order. Tick items in the same commit as the work, and log invented numbers in `docs/decisions.md`.

Already in the code when the notes were written (built in a hurry, never tested, so each still needs a check on a real ride):
- Zombie Bus can be ridden without ERG (menu or `E`; ↑ ↓ set the gradient).
- When someone falls off the bus, everyone else's target rises 5% per fallen rider, up to 15%.
- A shift-strength slider exists in Settings, the free-ride menu and the solo Slope mode.

## 2. Zombie Bus: make it a game you can lose

- [x] **The horde needs to be closer.** In ERG mode nobody ever saw it. Start it nearer, make it gain faster, and keep it in shot more of the time (camera or a rear-view inset). Done: it starts 30 m back (was 50) and can never be more than 70 m back (was 120); a crew riding above the event's neutral pulls away at 6 m/s per unit of compliance (was 15), and one riding below loses ground at 20 (was 15). A rear-view mirror on the bus shows the road behind whenever the horde is out, so it stays in sight at any gap. Per-level tuning comes with the next item.
- [x] **Game difficulty that means something.** Easy: about what the game is now. Normal: the crew should be close to failing by the end. Hard: doomed unless the crew earns gunships and other streak rewards. Host picks it in the lobby; tune horde speed, damage and fall-off thresholds per level. There is an existing difficulty setting to rework. Done: each level now also has a horde creep (it closes this many m/s whatever the crew does) and its own fall-off line. Tuned on simulated ERG crews over every bus plan: Easy never lost armour, Normal ended at about half armour (often less), Hard was doomed without streak air support and lived (just) with it, Nightmare was always lost. The lobby says what each level means.
- [ ] **Falling off is everyone's problem (built, untested).** Check the 5% per rider / 15% cap on a real ride and tune. Then make the team effort visible: show each rider's share of the crew's work as a percentage, who is carrying whom, and a clear banner while targets are raised. Think of more ways to make it a team effort (for example the fallen rider's missing watts are split across the crew, or the crew can choose who takes the extra). (waiting: see questions.md) Partly done: the crew list shows each rider's share of the crew's watts, 💪 marks whoever carries most while someone is off, and a red banner under the event header says who is off and the +% everyone else carries (the tick now sends `load`). Left: the 5% / 15% check on a real ride, and which other team mechanic to build.
- [x] **Cables from each trainer into the bus,** so it looks like the riders are powering it. Cables can glow or pulse with each rider's power. ## 4. Free ride feel

- [x] **Less wind noise at speed and on descents.** Quieter overall, and it should not keep climbing with speed. Done: wind level 0.5 → 0.28, full by 16 m/s (58 km/h) and no louder past it, and its pitch stops rising at 850 Hz, so a descent is a steady rush. About as loud as before at 30 km/h, roughly half at 60 km/h.
- [x] **More camera shake and lean on descents.** Done: shake starts sooner (9 m/s, 3% down) and is about twice as strong at full (16 m/s), and the camera takes half the rider's lean into bends, up to about 6° (the comfort test's limit). All in the debug panel (route → feel); "Calm camera" still turns it off.
- [x] **Drafting settings, and more drafting by default.** Longer reach with an exponential drop-off, a bit stronger on one wheel, and scaling with the size of the group: roughly 30% behind one rider, 50% behind two, up to about 95% deep in a pack of five, at a reference speed. Host settings in the group-ride lobby (off / realistic / strong). **Drafting must make the trainer feel easier,** not only the on-screen speed: reduce the gradient sent to the trainer by the drag saved, through `ergController.setGrade` only. Done: every moving rider ahead within 30 m counts, fully inside 1.5 m and fading by e every 6 m past it; the count picks the saving from the level's table (strong, the default: 30% on one wheel, 50% on two, 70, 85, then 95% with five ahead; realistic tops out at 60%; off is clean air). The host picks Off / Realistic / Strong in the group-ride lobby. The trainer gradient drops by the drag saved (`draftGrade`, via `ergController.setGrade` as before), so drafting is easier at the pedals too. Server and client share the numbers through road-vectors.json.
- [x] **Sort the routes into two clear sections.** "Real rides" for the routes cut from the crew's own recordings (Mondo, Full Gas Friday, Mount Coot-tha Back, Darren Smith, Metros, Nundah, Howick Street), and a separate section for the routes generated from map data. Done: the free-ride route picker shows "Real rides" (cut from the crew's own recordings) first, then "Classic climbs and roads" (built from map data). Routes built from a GPX or FIT recording carry `real: true` in content/routes/index.json; build_routes.py writes it.
